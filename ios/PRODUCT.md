# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

SwiftUI and Apple platform APIs over the existing Rust core, UniFFI, llama.cpp and static ONNX Runtime.
The user authorized implementation of the upstream maintainer's iOS requirements and pushing to their fork,
without an upstream PR. Source: upstream issue #1, README.md and AGENTS.md.

## Users and job

People researching without a connection, reading a local reference library or their own documents, and
checking the evidence behind an answer. Answers may be wrong; cited passages must remain easy to open.

## Constraints

No runtime network code, analytics or crash SDKs. Browser download and Files import. Redistributable models
and content only. Model choice uses available process memory. Physical-iPhone accuracy gates and separate
cool-phone speed checks precede release. iOS does not block Android delivery.

## Brand and voice

Commonplace, optionally Commonplace for iOS. Preserve the established Pocket Field Notes identity, the
existing factual copy and honest uncertainty. Use native navigation, controls, accessibility and share sheets.
